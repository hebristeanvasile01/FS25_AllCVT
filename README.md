# All CVT – FS25

No more gears: give any vehicle a **CVT automatic transmission**, and choose per vehicle.
Manual on your Fendt, CVT on your John Deere: your choice.

![icon](icon_preview.png)

## Features
- **CVT for every vehicle**: tractors, trucks, cars and machines with a manual or powershift gearbox get a stepless CVT
- **Switch per vehicle**: `Alt + G` in the vehicle turns CVT on or off (stand still)
- **Saved**: your choice per vehicle is kept in the savegame
- The speed range stays the same, from the lowest to the highest gear
- Smooth driving, no shifting, no ranges
- Vehicles that already have a CVT are not changed
- Works for base game, DLC and mod vehicles

## Controls
| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Alt + G` | CVT on / off for the vehicle you drive (stand still) |

## Console
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `cvtInfo` | Transmission of your vehicle |
| `cvtDefault on/off` | CVT on or off for vehicles you never switched (after a restart) |

## Install
Download **FS25_AllCVT.zip** from the [latest release](../../releases/latest) and put it in your `mods` folder. Do not unzip it.
